Share what helps. Find what you need.

A kinder way to exchange T1D supplies.

This community webapp connects people with Type‑1 diabetic supplies (insulin, CGMs, pumps, test strips, syringes, ketone sticks, Baqsimi, etc.) to those who need them—whether for exchange, sale at fair prices, or free.

It is not for profit. The goal is to reduce waste, help with rising costs, and support people who may be switching devices, running low on supplies, or have items nearing expiration that others can still use.

Find nearby offers with a city/area or ZIP code—no coordinates or street address needed. Matches are approximate. Contact details are shared only after the supply owner approves a connection request.
